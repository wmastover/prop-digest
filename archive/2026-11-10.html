Gleeson warns that first-time buyer scheme will stall sales

Gleeson Homes says the wait for Your First Home will hold up new-build sales. Buyers are sitting tight until the scheme is up and running, rather than committing to a new-build purchase now. Why it matters: if Gleeson is right, the scheme becomes a talking point before it becomes a help, and new-build sales could slow while everyone waits to see the rules. 📊 The numbers

Bridging lending rises as landlords buy investment property

Contributors to the Bridging Trends index lent almost £182 million in the three months to September, and the rise is put down to landlords buying investment property. Bridging is short-term finance, usually used to cover a gap until longer-term funding arrives, so this is people moving fast rather than waiting for a standard mortgage. My take: it's a sign some investors are still buying, even if the summary doesn't say how sharp the rise is. The Negotiator
