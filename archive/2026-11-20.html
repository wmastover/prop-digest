Friday, and the BestAgent row has claimed another scalp. Plus a forecast that says deals are about to bounce, which sounds lovely until you remember who's making it. Coffee's hot.

The big one

Yopa boss quits BestAgent advisory board after listings row

Verona Frankish boss Charlie Lamdin has stepped down from BestAgent's advisory board. It follows several days of criticism from estate agents over how Yopa used their listings, and a copyright argument that's been rattling around the trade. Why it matters: this is a rare crack in the challenger camp, and it puts the spotlight back on whether listing data can be treated as anyone's property. Watch whether BestAgent's other backers follow him out. The Negotiator

📊 The numbers

Momentum slows, but buyers get some breathing room

Kate Faulkner says sales activity is slowing, which is bad news for sellers, while affordability has improved for buyers. Why it matters: sellers hoping for a bidding war will be disappointed, and buyers with a bit of cash have some room to negotiate again. The Negotiator

Rental supply hits a seven-year high and outruns demand

Nick Huntley of TwentyEA says the Renters' Rights Act hasn't yet cut the amount of rental stock on the market, but demand has dropped. Why it matters: more homes chasing fewer tenants is the opposite of what landlords were promised, and it will squeeze rent growth if it holds. The Negotiator

🗣️ Who said what

Zoopla: start the downsizing chat before the repairs bill lands

Richard Donnell of Zoopla says families should start talking about downsizing before costly repairs force the decision. He's describing what he calls the 'property sandwich generation', squeezed between ageing parents and their own kids. Why it matters: a useful pitch for agents with older clients, if you can get the conversation started early. The Negotiator

Adam Day of eXp: transactions to rebound 6.7% next year

Adam Day of eXp UK expects the number of property deals to rise 6.7% next year, after a drop in transaction figures. Forecasts are cheap, and this one is only as good as its assumptions. My take: a bounce is plausible, but I'd want to see the mortgage rate picture before I bet the bonus on it. The Negotiator

Renters are borrowing to pay deposits

Ben Grech, CEO of Reposit, says their analysis shows more tenants turning to credit to pay for a home. Why it matters: if deposits are going on credit cards, the arrears and affordability problems are about to get worse, not better. The Negotiator

Cleverly promises £100,000 off London new-builds

James Cleverly, the Tory candidate for London Mayor, says cutting red tape and taxes could knock £100,000 off new-build prices in the capital. My take: a big number with no mechanism attached yet. Mayoral pledges have a habit of quietly disappearing after polling day. The Negotiator
