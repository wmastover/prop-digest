The big one

Landlords plan rent hikes at the earliest opportunity

A poll of landlords since the Autumn Budget says they plan to raise rents by up to 10% as soon as they're allowed to. That's the sound of a market bracing for a fight, not one settling down. Why it matters: if you're letting to tenants or advising landlords, expect the rent conversation to get heated the moment the rules loosen. Plenty of landlords are clearly already planning around the Budget, so watch how quickly that intent shows up in actual asking rents. The Negotiator

🏛️ Rules and politics

Agents warned about Data Subject Access Requests

Paul Offley, Group Compliance Officer at The Guild of Property Professionals, says he's seen a rise in data requests from clients. Those requests can be a proper headache if you don't handle them properly. Why it matters: if your team treats a Data Subject Access Request as junk mail, you're asking for a complaint. Get the process written down before someone's inbox turns into a compliance case. The Negotiator

📊 The numbers

Tenants face tougher rent affordability in many areas

London still needs the highest salary to cover rent, according to Propertymark's Kim Lidbury. The affordability squeeze is showing up well beyond the capital too. Why it matters: affordability is the figure that decides how long a tenant can stay put and how much pushback a rent rise gets. The Negotiator

🏢 Portals and big agencies

Sowerbys acquires Birds Estate Agents' lettings portfolio

Sowerbys has bought the lettings portfolio of Birds Estate Agents. Ed Tyman, Sowerbys' Lettings and Operations Director, says the deal will keep things steady for Birds' landlords and tenants. Why it matters: another lettings book changing hands, and the buyer's promise is continuity. Judge that by whether the landlords stay put. The Negotiator

🗣️ Who said what

Valuation reports are too time consuming, say agents

Heather Staff of agency software Street Group says agents struggle to produce valuation reports easily, and staff time is getting eaten up. Honestly, that's no shock to anyone who's written one at 9pm. Why it matters: if valuations are slow, the tech that speeds them up becomes a selling point. The Negotiator

🌍 Elsewhere

IRES REIT accepts Barings' €731m takeover offer

The board of Ireland's biggest private residential landlord has unanimously recommended Barings' offer. Shareholders are due 138.6c (115.9p) in cash per share. Irish rather than UK, but big institutional rental money moving is worth noting. Property Week

Renaker unveils plans for 54-storey Manchester residential tower

Renaker's plan is for 556 one- and two-bedroom apartments as part of the Greengate cluster of city centre skyscrapers. It's a planning proposal, not a finished build. Manchester keeps getting taller. Property Week
