The big one

BestAgent says basic listing details aren't anyone's copyright

BestAgent founder Charlie Lamdin is defending his site's use of estate agency property details, insisting the basic information isn't anyone's copyright. The summary only gives his side, so we don't know yet what agents or portals are arguing back. Why it matters: if the bare facts are fair game, agents' stock becomes raw material for anyone with a scraper and a business plan. Watch whether this turns into a legal fight or just a shouting match on LinkedIn. The Negotiator

🏛️ Rules and politics

Landlords sell up after Budget tax hikes

Roma Sharma of Rushbrook says landlords are putting tenanted homes on the market faster than before. Why it matters: the Budget tax changes are now showing up in stock levels, which is exactly the exodus agents were warning about. Expect more instructions on tenanted properties, and more sales that come with sitting tenants. The Negotiator

📊 The numbers

Over-65s sit on £2.25tn of property wealth, but hardly anyone moves

Savills says households aged 65 and over hold £2.25tn of the UK's £4.29tn owner-occupied equity. Relatively few are downsizing, even though they released £20.8bn of equity last year. Why it matters: the wealth is there, but people aren't moving, so the sellers agents need aren't coming out of the woodwork on their own. Property Industry EYE

🗣️ Who said what

Lenders told to give non-standard borrowers a proper hearing

Aaron Shinwell of Nottingham Building Society says non-standard borrowers should get a chance to explain their income. Fair enough, and it's the kind of thing brokers have been saying for years. The question is whether lenders actually change their process or just write a nicer policy document. The Negotiator

Parents are increasingly buying kids million-pound homes outright

Mark Pollack of Aston Chase says some parents are buying homes outright for their children, with gifts running into the millions. Take that as one agent's observation, not a market trend with numbers behind it. It's still a nice reminder that the Bank of Mum and Dad is now a serious buyer. The Negotiator

🥊 Challengers

Rent payers can now earn Avios for paying on time

Fintech platform Payr has launched a tool that gives tenants Avios points for every month their rent goes in on time. It's a cute way to make rent feel less like dead money. Why it matters: if tenants start treating their landlord's bank details as a loyalty scheme, rent payment data gets more valuable, and so does whoever holds it. The Negotiator

🌍 Elsewhere

GSA buys 75% of Sanctuary student portfolio in £600m JV

The portfolio has 12 student accommodation assets, mostly in London zone one, plus some in Manchester and Liverpool. Commercial, but student housing keeps pulling in big money. Property Week

GPE sells Clifford Chance's London HQ to Rothesay for £422.3m

The City office is fully let to Clifford Chance until 2046, which is a long lease and a safe-looking income. Big-ticket, but nothing for the residential desk to act on. Property Week
